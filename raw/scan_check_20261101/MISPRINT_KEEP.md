# Misprints left as printed in the corrected French edition (decision 'keep' or 'unclear' of the misprint pass)

- MISPRINTS:2 (I p. 84), keep: Probably (1.1.11) (Spec(A/𝔞) identified with V(𝔞) for any ideal 𝔞), (1.1.12) being only its case 𝔞=𝔑; but the content of (1.1.11) and (1.1.12) is not on this page and the reference is not in the authors' errata, so the number cannot be checked here.
- MISPRINTS:76 (I p. 166), keep: The printed V(𝔞)=W∩F is false (F=W∩V(𝔞), and V(𝔞) may have points outside W); the argument needs, for x∈W, 𝔞⊂𝔪_x ⟺ x∈V(𝔞) ⟺ x∈F. The intended wording is not determined (V(𝔞) and F interchanged, or a rephrasing), and the line is not in the authors' errata.
- MISPRINTS:79 (I p. 169), unclear: The sentence lies in ega1-9-fr.tex before the marker of p. 170, not in the page text given, so no exact edit can be placed here. The correct reading is '𝒪_Z-Module' (a sheaf of modules); the TeX keeps '𝒪_Z-module'.
- MISPRINTS:59 (I p. 202), keep: 'appliqué' can agree with M^Δ (the functor: 'M^Δ, appliqué à la suite exacte'); the agreement with 'la propriété' is not certain, so this is not a sure slip.
- MISPRINTS:121 (II p. 9), keep: U is not defined in this proof; with S affine, Γ(S,B̃)=B. The U may echo the open U of the definition (I, 9.6.2), here U=S, so a misprint is not certain.
- MISPRINTS:142 (II p. 10), keep: Under (1.4.3) ℬ is an arbitrary quasi-coherent 𝒪_S-Algèbre, so nothing makes B of finite type (and the proof does not use it); but the intended wording and reference (perhaps I, 1.4.3) cannot be determined.
- MISPRINTS:381 (III p. 102), unclear: Not found: the corrected edition gives (2.2.4) with the hypothesis 'le support de Im(F -> G) soit propre sur Y' and a proof through G_1 = Im(F -> G) and the reduction to H = 0; the sentence 'Supp(G) ... contenu dans Supp(F) ∩ Supp(H)' does not occur in it, so there is nothing to edit. (In the printed proof the union is the correct reading: G_x = 0 wherever F_x = H_x = 0.)
- MISPRINTS:509 (III p. 210), keep: The print's (1.4.14.5) cites nothing (no such item in EGA III; EGA I has no 4.14.5), and the edition's (I, 4.14.5) is only a conjecture. The fact used (a quasi-coherent sheaf on an affine whose module is flat is flat over the base) is, per the checkers' note to MISPRINTS:484, Lemme (1.4.15.5) of EGA III; that target cannot be checked from this batch. Settle together with 484.
- MISPRINTS:484 (III p. 212), keep: Same defective (1.4.14.5) as p. 210 (MISPRINTS:509). The proposed (1.4.15.5) (Lemme, ega3-1-fr.tex) is plausible, but its content is not on this page or elsewhere in this batch, so it is not verified here; the edition still has the conjectural (I, 4.14.5).
- MISPRINTS:581 (0\textsubscript{IV-1} p. 100), keep: (19.5.4.1) is a paragraph of the proof of (19.5.4), not a homomorphism; the homomorphism meant is that of (19.5.7)(ii), but its number (proposed 19.5.7.1) is not visible in this batch (the statement of 19.5.7 is on p. 99).
- MISPRINTS:572 (0\textsubscript{IV-1} p. 125), keep: Not a misprint: d_{B/A}=p_1−p_2 is the print's deliberate convention, used consistently (x⊗1−1⊗x in the proof of 20.4.7, p_2=p_1−d in that of 20.4.8); either sign gives a derivation. p_2−p_1 is the authors' later change of convention (Err_IV 12, under B) Modifications de texte). If the edition adopts the authors' text modifications, apply 572, 573 and 590 together.
- MISPRINTS:573 (0\textsubscript{IV-1} p. 125), keep: Consistent with the print's d_{B/A}=p_1−p_2 (see MISPRINTS:572); the change is the authors' Err_IV 12 change of convention, not a misprint.
- MISPRINTS:590 (0\textsubscript{IV-1} p. 126), keep: p_2=p_1−d_{B/A} follows from the print's definition d=p_1−p_2 (see MISPRINTS:572); p_1=p_2−d is the authors' Err_IV 13 change of convention, not a misprint.
- MISPRINTS:592 (0\textsubscript{IV-1} p. 127), keep: The authors' replacement of the whole item (iv) (Err_IV 14, B) Modifications de texte) generalizes it; the printed (iv) is correct under its hypothesis that A is integrally closed. Not a misprint.
- MISPRINTS:696 (IV-2 p. 57), keep: Ass(F tensor G) without the subscript k is an abbreviation: the tensor product over k is meant and unambiguous. Not a certain misprint.
- MISPRINTS:887 (IV-3 p. 206), keep: No U appears in the visible part of (14.3.6) or its proof, and Y'' is used as a Y-scheme, so Y may be meant; but U may denote an affine open neighbourhood of y as in (14.3.3.1, (i)) and (8.10.2). The page does not settle it; no erratum.
- MISPRINTS:916 (IV-3 p. 242), unclear: The place is in ega4-15-terminal-fr.tex before its first page marker (IV-3 243), outside the page text given for IV-3 242, so no exact edit can be checked. The correction itself is certain: in (15.7.2) c) the valuation ring dominates O_y (proof: 'A domine aussi O_y, donc par hypothèse'); the corrected edition should read 'dominant O_y domine'.
- MISPRINTS:1140 (IV-4 p. 295), keep: The print gives the number (21.10.13) to both the lemma (p. 294) and this proposition (p. 295); any new number would be an editorial choice, not a certain reading. The label IV.21.10.13bis already separates the references.
- MISPRINTS:947 (IV-4 p. 55), keep: n is not defined in (16.11.1)-(16.11.3); the sense is that D_p commutes with multiplication by sections only for p = 0 (D_0 = identity). The authors may use n loosely for the order |p| of D_p; whether to read p = 0 or |p| = 0 is not settled.
- MISPRINTS:984 (IV-4 p. 96), keep: The diagram and the exact sequence are both numbered (17.13.9.1). The later reference 'la situation de (17.13.9.1)' is to the diagram and (17.13.9.2) is taken by tau, so the number the sequence should carry cannot be read off; a new number would be an editorial invention. Label keys stay unchanged.
- MISPRINTS:1026 (IV-4 p. 160), keep: f_j is defined nowhere; meant is the restriction of f to X_{2j+1}. Whether the intended notation is f or f_{2j+1} (the index j is wrong in either reading) is not settled.
